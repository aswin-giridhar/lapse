# Residential Lease — Unit 4B, Brightwater Court (excerpt)
Landlord: Brightwater Property Management | Term: 12 months from May 1, 2026

## Section 11 — Maintenance requests and notice
All maintenance and repair requests must be submitted **in writing
through the resident portal** at brightwater.example/residents, or by
letter delivered to the management office. **Requests made by telephone
or text message are for convenience only and do not constitute written
notice under this lease or under applicable law.**

## Section 12 — Landlord response
Following written notice of a condition affecting habitability, Landlord
shall commence repair within fourteen (14) days. Where Landlord fails to
commence repair within that period, Tenant may pursue the remedies
available under state law, which may include repair-and-deduct.
