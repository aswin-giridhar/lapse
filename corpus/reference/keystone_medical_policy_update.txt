KEYSTONE MUTUAL HEALTH
MEDICAL POLICY BULLETIN MP-2026-03
Effective date: March 1, 2026
Distribution: members, contracted providers

SUBJECT: Advanced imaging for persistent lumbar radiculopathy

Effective March 1, 2026, MRI of the lumbar spine without contrast
(CPT 72148) is reclassified from INVESTIGATIONAL to MEDICALLY NECESSARY
when performed for persistent lumbar radiculopathy with documented
symptom duration of six weeks or greater and failure of conservative
management.

This bulletin supersedes the prior classification of CPT 72148 for this
indication in Medical Policy MP-2024-11.
