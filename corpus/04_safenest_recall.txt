IMPORTANT SAFETY RECALL NOTICE
SafeNest Juvenile Products, Inc.
Issued: August 20, 2026
Recall reference: SN-2026-04

AFFECTED PRODUCT
SafeNest Horizon 360 convertible car seat
Model numbers HZ360-B, HZ360-G
Manufactured between January 2025 and April 2026

HAZARD
The harness adjuster can loosen during normal use, allowing the harness
to exceed the specified slack tolerance. This may reduce restraint
effectiveness in a collision.

REMEDY
SafeNest will provide a free replacement harness assembly and, on
request, a free installation appointment at an authorised fitting
centre. There is no charge to the consumer for this remedy.

The free remedy programme is available for 90 days from the date of this
notice. After that date, replacement assemblies remain available for
purchase at the then-current retail price.

To register: safenest.example/recall or 1-800-555-0142
