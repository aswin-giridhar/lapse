# Statement of Work — Meridian Labs Customer Portal
Between: Meridian Labs, Inc. ("Client") and A. Nair ("Contractor")
Executed: July 6, 2026 | Fixed fee: $28,000

## Exhibit A — Deliverables in scope
1. Customer portal dashboard, desktop layout
2. Authentication and session management
3. Data table and filtering components
4. **Responsive breakpoints for tablet and mobile viewports across all
   delivered screens**
5. Two rounds of visual revision per delivered screen

## Exhibit B — Expressly out of scope
- Native mobile applications (iOS / Android)
- Backend API development beyond the documented integration points
- Content and copywriting
- Accessibility audit and remediation

## Section 6 — Changes to scope
Work requested by Client that falls outside Exhibit A constitutes a
change order and is billed at $185/hour. **Contractor must notify Client
in writing that requested work is out of scope within ten (10) business
days of the request.** Absent such notice within that period, the
requested work is deemed included within the fixed fee and no additional
charge may be made for it.
