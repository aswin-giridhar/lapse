# Keystone Mutual Health — Certificate of Coverage (excerpt)
Plan: Keystone Select PPO 2026 | Group: 44712

## Section 7.2 — Investigational and experimental services
Services classified as investigational or experimental for the submitted
diagnosis are not eligible expenses. Classification is determined by the
Plan's published Medical Policy, as amended from time to time. **The
Medical Policy in effect on the date of service governs.**

## Section 9.1 — Your right to a review of an adverse determination
If a claim is denied in whole or in part, you may request an internal
review of that determination. A request for internal review must be
received **within 180 days of the date of the notice of adverse benefit
determination.** Requests received after that period will not be
considered and the determination becomes final.

## Section 9.4 — What to include
A request for review should identify the claim number, state the basis
for disagreement, and may include additional documentation, including
published Plan Medical Policy in effect on the date of service.
