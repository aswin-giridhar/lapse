# Northgate Electronics — Price Protection Policy
Effective January 2026

If the price of an item you purchased from Northgate Electronics falls within
**thirty (30) calendar days of your order date**, you may request a refund of
the difference between the price you paid and the lower price.

Requests must be made within the 30-day period. Requests made after the
period has closed cannot be honoured, regardless of the size of the
difference.

Exclusions: clearance and open-box items, bundle pricing, third-party
marketplace sellers, and price differences arising from promotional codes
applied at checkout.

To request price protection: northgate.example/price-protection or any store
service desk, with your order number.
